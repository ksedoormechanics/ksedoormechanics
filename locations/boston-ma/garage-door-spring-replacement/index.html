Signs of Garage Door Spring Problems

A loud bang

A sharp, gunshot-like noise from the garage often means a spring has snapped.

Door will not open, or only a little

The door lifts a foot or two, or not at all.

Unusually heavy door

The opener strains or hums, or the door will not stay halfway open.

Visible gap in the spring

A torsion spring split into two coils, or a stretched, distorted extension spring.

Uneven or crooked travel

One side lifts faster, or the door tilts.

Slack or piled cables

Cables loose at the bottom or off the drums.

These symptoms can also come from cables, drums, tracks or the opener, so professional diagnosis is recommended. Do not touch, adjust or try to release a spring or cable. Stop using the door; running the opener against a broken spring can snap cables, bend tracks or damage the opener.

Spring Repair vs. Replacement

Inspection finds what has failed. A fatigued or broken spring is normally replaced, not repaired; we do not guarantee any spring can be repaired. Related hardware, such as cables, drums, bearings or brackets, may also need replacing if worn or damaged by the failure. Replacement recommendations come only after professional inspection. Boston Spring Replacement FAQs

Do you offer garage door spring replacement in Boston?

Yes. KSE Door Mechanics serves Boston, South Boston and East Boston, MA from its business address in Needham. Call (833) 877-2561 with your address and door details to confirm coverage and arrange an inspection.

How can I tell if my garage door spring is broken?

Common signs are a loud bang, a visible gap in a torsion spring, a door that feels very heavy or will only lift a short way, uneven or crooked travel, slack cables, and an opener that hums or strains. Other faults can look similar, so a technician should confirm.

Can a garage door spring be repaired?

Garage door springs are generally replaced, not repaired, because a spring that has fatigued or broken cannot be reliably mended. Whether you need one spring or a pair, and any related parts, depends on an inspection.

Do you replace torsion springs?

Yes, torsion spring replacement is part of our service. The replacement must match the door's weight, size and the manufacturer's specifications. See torsion spring replacement.

Do you replace extension springs?

Yes, extension spring replacement is offered for compatible doors, along with the safety cables that go with them. See extension spring replacement.

Can I open my garage door with a broken spring?

We advise against it. Without the spring's counterbalance the door can be very heavy and can drop or jam, and running the opener against it can strip the opener and snap cables. Keep clear of the door and call for service.

Should both springs be replaced if only one broke?

On a two-spring torsion system, technicians often recommend replacing both because they share the same age and wear, but it depends on the inspection and matching specifications. You can ask your technician to explain the reason for the recommendation.

How long do garage door springs last?

There is no fixed lifespan. Springs are rated for a number of open-and-close cycles, standard ratings are commonly around 10,000 cycles and higher-cycle springs are available. Use, door weight, corrosion and maintenance all matter.

What does a spring replacement quote include?
