How Boston Weather Affects Garage Doors

Slush, snow and road salt. These collect near the threshold and on photo-eye lenses, which commonly makes a door reverse or refuse to close. Wipe the lenses gently and clear the beam path.

Freeze and thaw. Ice can bond the bottom seal to the floor, and cold thickens lubricant. If the door sticks, don't force it, since pulling can tear the seal or strain the opener and door. See weatherstripping.

Moisture and corrosion. Damp garages, especially near the water, can rust springs, cables and hardware over time. Visible rust is worth a look during maintenance.
