What Garage Door Cables Do

Garage doors are heavy. Springs counterbalance that weight, and cables carry the lifting force between the springs and the door so it moves evenly. Drums or pulleys guide the cables, while tracks and rollers keep the door on its path.

Garage Door Cable Repair FAQs

What are common signs of a damaged garage door cable?

Common signs are frayed or broken strands, a crooked or uneven door, a door that is hard to open or close, a loose or displaced cable, a door that stops working normally, and unusual noise near the cable drums or lifting hardware. These symptoms can have several causes, so a technician needs to diagnose the actual problem.

Is it safe to use a garage door with a damaged cable?

No. Cables work with a spring system and carry a heavy door, so a damaged cable can make the door unstable. Stop using the door, do not try to lift or force it, keep children, pets and vehicles clear, and request professional service.

Can I repair or reconnect a garage door cable myself?

We strongly advise against it. Cables and drums are tied to a high-tension spring system, and videos showing drum rewinding or clamping a door are risky. Do not touch, reposition or loosen anything. Call a professional to inspect and service the door.

What causes garage door cables to wear or break?

Daily use, abrasion against hardware, a cable rubbing out of its path, corrosion from moisture and winter road salt, kinks, and problems elsewhere in the system such as springs, tracks or balance. Because the cause matters, a good repair looks beyond the cable alone.

Do garage door cables work with the spring system?

Yes. On torsion systems, lift cables wind on drums at the spring shaft. On extension systems, cables run over pulleys, and a separate safety cable may run through each spring. Springs balance the door's weight, and cables transfer that lifting force to the door.

Does cable repair include checking related hardware?

Our service scope includes reviewing related components such as drums or pulleys, brackets, tracks, rollers, spring condition, alignment and balance, because a damaged cable can signal another issue. Confirm the exact scope for your door when you call.
