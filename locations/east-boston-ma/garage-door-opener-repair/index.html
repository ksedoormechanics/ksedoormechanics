What Can You Safely Check Before Calling?

  • Replace remote or keypad batteries.
  • Confirm the opener is plugged in, any GFCI outlet has not tripped and the breaker is on.
  • Check that the wall control is not in a locked mode.
  • Gently wipe photo-eye lenses near the bottom of the tracks; winter slush and road salt can dirty them. The indicator lights should be steady, not blinking.
  • Note any blinking light pattern on the opener and when the problem happens.

Do not open the opener housing, handle wiring, bypass sensors or adjust springs, cables or brackets. A broken spring makes any opener look faulty, and a new opener will not fix it.

Why Does the Opener Run but the Door Not Move?

A running motor with a stationary door can mean a worn drive gear, a disconnected trolley or a disengaged release. It can also mean a broken spring or binding door, where the opener is not the real problem. Do not force the door or pull the release if the door is open or unstable. Why does my garage door opener run but the door does not move?

A running motor with a stationary door can point to a worn drive gear, a disconnected trolley or a disengaged release. It can also mean the door itself is blocked by a broken spring, which a new opener will not fix. Do not force the door; have a technician inspect it.

Why does my garage door reverse right after closing?

This is commonly caused by safety sensors (photo-eyes) that are dirty, bumped out of alignment or blocked. You can gently wipe the lenses and check that the indicator lights are steady. Never bypass the sensors. If the problem continues, a technician should inspect them.

Why does the wall button work but the remote does not?

The likely causes are dead remote or keypad batteries, lost programming, signal interference or a failed remote. If batteries do not help, the remote may need reprogramming under the manufacturer's procedure or replacing.

What should I check if my garage door opener has no power?

Check that the opener is plugged in, that any GFCI outlet has not tripped and that the circuit breaker is on. Do not open the opener housing or touch wiring. If power is present and the opener is still dead, request professional diagnosis.

Why does my garage door opener work intermittently?

Intermittent operation can come from weak batteries, a locked wall control, sensor alignment, a loose connection, worn parts or a control board fault. Note when it fails and any blinking light patterns so the technician can narrow it down.

Can a broken spring make my opener seem broken?

Yes. A broken spring makes the door very heavy, so the opener may strain, stop partway or only hum. Replacing the opener will not solve a spring problem. Stop using the door and have the spring system inspected.

Should I keep using an opener that makes unusual noises?

Not if the noise is new, loud or paired with jerking, stopping or crooked movement. Minor noise may only need inspection. Grinding, clicking or straining can mean worn gears or a door problem, so schedule an inspection.

Is it worth repairing a garage door opener or should I replace it?

It depends on opener age, the failed part, parts availability, compatibility, safety features and repair cost compared with replacement. A technician can compare options after inspection. Repair is not always possible, and replacement is not always necessary.
