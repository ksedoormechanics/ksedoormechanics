Do you repair broken garage door springs?

Yes. Spring problems are common, and replacement should be done by trained professionals because springs hold stored energy. Stop using the door if you suspect a broken spring and call us. See spring replacement.

Can you repair garage door openers?

Yes. We repair openers, remotes, keypads and safety sensors, and can advise on when replacement makes sense. See garage door opener repair.

Do you install replacement garage doors?

Yes. We install replacement and new doors, starting with measurements, hardware compatibility and opener checks. See garage door installation.

Can you service shared garages?

Often, but the details matter. Shared or multi-unit garages involve access, parking, ownership and sometimes association or landlord approval. Tell us about the setup when you call and we’ll explain what we can do.

Who can authorize work on a rented or shared garage door?

Usually the owner, landlord or building association decides. If you rent or live in a condo, check who is responsible first. We can describe the problem in writing for them, but we perform work only when it’s authorized.

What should I do if my cable or spring breaks right now?
