South Boston Garage Door Installation FAQs

What should I consider when choosing a new garage door?

Consider opening size, material, insulation needs, appearance, how the garage is used, existing tracks and springs, and opener compatibility. Attached garages and shared buildings may add constraints, so a professional measurement matters before you order.

What garage door styles are available?

Common residential styles include traditional raised-panel, carriage-house, modern flush and full-view designs in steel or other materials. Availability depends on the product lines we can supply, so ask us which styles fit your opening.

Can my existing opener work with a new door?

Often, but not always. The opener must suit the new door's weight and balance, and its safety sensors must work. We check this at assessment. We never raise opener force to cover an unbalanced door.

Does installation include removing the old door?

Removal depends on the agreed scope. Confirm in advance whether old door removal and hauling are included, along with track, spring and opener work, so there are no surprises on installation day.

How long does garage door installation take?

It depends on door size, the existing opening, hardware and opener work. We do not promise fixed times or same-day installation. We confirm a timeline after we inspect your garage.

How much does a new garage door cost in South Boston?

Cost depends on size, material, insulation, windows, hardware, springs, opener needs and site conditions. We do not post prices online, so call (833) 877-2561 after you know your door size for an estimate.

Do I need a permit to replace a garage door?

Requirements depend on the property and the scope of work. Check with the City of Boston's building department, and ask your landlord, condo association or property manager if the garage is shared or part of a multi-unit building.

How should I prepare for an installation appointment?

Clear vehicles and stored items from the door area, keep children and pets away, make sure the opener's power outlet is accessible, and arrange building or shared-garage access in advance. Do not remove springs, cables or brackets yourself.
