Common Garage Door Problems in South Boston

Cold winters, freeze-thaw cycles, road salt and coastal moisture all put wear on springs, cables and hardware. These are the problems we see most often, and what each usually involves.

Broken garage door springs

Springs fail under high tension. A door that is suddenly too heavy to lift, or stuck, often has a broken spring. Do not attempt to handle the spring yourself.

Garage door opener failure

Motors, circuit boards, gears and power connections can fail, leaving the remote or wall control without a response.

Garage door refusing to close

Often caused by safety sensors that are misaligned, dirty or blocked. Wiring and opener logic can also be involved.

Damaged cables

Lift cables can fray, rust or slip off their drums. Damaged cables are a reason to stop using the door until it is inspected.

Misaligned tracks

Loose hardware or impact can move tracks out of line and cause rollers to bind or jump the track.

Noisy garage doors

Dry or worn rollers, hinges and bearings cause squeaking, popping or grinding. Noise sometimes signals an alignment or balance problem.

Worn rollers

Rollers wear over time. Worn rollers can cause jerky movement and extra strain on the opener.

Routine maintenance

Lubrication, hardware checks and safety tests prevent many of the problems above.

Why Local Conditions Matter for South Boston Garage Doors

Winter weather and moisture affect a garage door throughout the year. Each factor below can contribute to wear, and our technicians look for these during an inspection.

  • Freeze-thaw cycles can stress springs, cables and seals.
  • Snow and ice can block sensors and push against the bottom seal.
  • Road salt and moisture can corrode hardware, brackets and wiring.
  • Heavy daily use wears rollers, hinges and openers faster on busy doors.

Spring work is for trained technicians

Torsion and extension springs store lethal tension. Do not try to wind, adjust, release or replace a spring yourself, and do not stand under a door that is held up by a damaged spring. Call us for a safe assessment.
