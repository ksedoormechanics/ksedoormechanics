Common garage door problems

Door won't open

Often a broken spring, dead remote battery or misaligned sensor. If the opener hums but the door stays shut, check for a broken spring.

Door won't close

Dirty or misaligned safety sensors are the usual cause. Wipe the lenses and check the indicator lights.

Off track or uneven

Bent tracks, worn rollers or a snapped cable. Stop using the door.

Grinding or squeaking

Worn rollers, dry hinges or an unbalanced door.

Safety first

Springs, cables and bottom brackets are under extreme tension. Do not adjust or remove them yourself. Call a professional.

Garage door FAQs

Why won't my garage door open?

Common causes are a broken spring, a dead remote battery, a disconnected opener or misaligned sensors. If you see a gap in the spring, don't force the door.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

Replacing a spring, cable or roller is usually enough. Replacement is worth considering for heavily damaged panels or a door that's repeatedly failing.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

An annual inspection is a good habit. It catches worn springs, cables and sensors before they fail.
