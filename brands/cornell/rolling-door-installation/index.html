Quick answer: We identify the Cornell model and age first, because parts, remotes and hardware differ by model and similar-looking parts may not fit. Photos and labels help us pre-check compatibility.

Note the model label, size or type and age, and send photos of the whole setup and any labels.

Testing, Safety and Handover

Quick answer: Commercial doors are heavy and often under high spring or motor loads, and some are fire-rated. Keep clear of the opening and don't force a jammed door.

  • Operation testing after service

  • Advice on what to watch for
