Haas Door Maintenance and Safety Checks

Quick answer: Regular checks help catch wear early but can't prevent every failure. Springs, cables, drums and bottom brackets are under tension and are not for DIY handling.

  • Watch for noise, binding or uneven movement
  • Look at springs, cables and rollers from a safe distance
  • Check weatherseals for wear
  • Test the opener's safety sensors and reversal
  • Schedule periodic professional inspection
  • Never adjust springs, cables or bottom brackets yourself
