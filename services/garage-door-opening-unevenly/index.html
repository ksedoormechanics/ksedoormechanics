Why is my garage door opening unevenly?

A garage door opens unevenly when its two sides do not lift together. The usual reasons are a slipped or unevenly worn lift cable, a cable drum problem, a worn or binding roller, a track out of position, or a spring or balance fault. One symptom can have several causes, so only an inspection can name the part. Avoid running a visibly crooked door repeatedly. Call KSE Door Mechanics at (833) 877-2561.

Stop using the door if you see any of these

The door tilts sharply, a cable is frayed, slack or off its drum, a roller is out of the track, a spring looks broken, the door binds or moves unpredictably, or part of it hangs unsupported. Keep people and pets away, never stand under it, do not force the opener, and do not try to lift it by hand.

What you can safely check

  • Watch from a safe position and note which side rises first.
  • Look for visibly loose or frayed cables without touching them.
  • Check from a distance for rollers that look out of place.
  • Note new grinding or scraping sounds.
  • Look for obstructions only while the door is stationary.
  • Take a photo of the crooked position, then call.

Please do not lift the door by hand, release or adjust springs, disconnect or reposition cables or drums, loosen brackets, push rollers into tracks, force the door straight, or raise opener force.

Garage door opening unevenly: FAQs

Why is my garage door opening unevenly?

One side is getting more lift, or less resistance, than the other. Common contributors are a slipped or unevenly worn lift cable, a cable drum problem, a worn or binding roller, a track out of position, or a spring or balance problem. The symptom does not name the part, so a technician needs to inspect the whole system.

Why does one side of my garage door rise faster?

Usually the lower side is carrying more load or fighting more friction. That can come from unequal cable travel, a drum that is not turning in step with the shaft, a roller dragging in its track, or counterbalance that is not shared evenly. Stop using the door and have it inspected.

Why does my garage door open crooked?

A crooked lift means the two sides are not moving together. Causes include cable slack on one side, a binding roller, a bent or shifted track, a hinge or bracket problem, or a spring issue. Several different faults can look identical from the floor.

Can lift cables cause uneven garage door opening?

Yes. If one cable is slack, displaced, worn or has slipped on its drum, that side loses support and the door can rise at an angle. Cables are under high tension, so do not touch or reposition them. Call a technician.

Can a broken spring make a garage door open unevenly?

It can. A broken or weakened spring changes how weight is carried, and the door may lift at an angle, feel heavy, or stop short. But uneven lifting does not always mean a spring failure, so a spring should be confirmed by inspection, not assumed.

Can worn rollers or a misaligned track cause crooked lifting?

Yes. A roller that binds, or a track that is out of plumb or loose at its mounting, adds drag on one side. The door can then hesitate, scrape or tilt as it rises. Do not push rollers back into the track yourself.

Why is my garage door level when closed but crooked while opening?

A closed door rests on the floor, so it can look square while the cables, drums or rollers are already uneven. The problem only shows once the door is moving under load. Please do not cycle the door repeatedly to test it.

Why did my garage door start opening crooked after a spring or cable replacement?

Cables, drums and spring settings must be matched side to side. If any of them were set unevenly, or if tracks are mounted at different heights, the door can tilt even with new parts. Contact the installer or call KSE for an inspection.

Is uneven opening the same as an off-track door?

No, but they are related. Uneven opening is a symptom. An off-track door has a roller outside the track and can be unstable or unsupported. If a roller is out of the track, do not operate the door.

Is it safe to use a garage door that opens unevenly?

Not repeatedly. A crooked door can bind, strain the opener, damage cables or tracks, or become unstable. If it tilts sharply, a cable looks slack or frayed, or a roller is out of the track, stop using it and keep people clear.

Does uneven opening always require spring replacement?

No. Cables, drums, rollers, tracks, hinges and balance can each cause it. A technician checks the entire system first and recommends only what the inspection supports.
