Common garage door problems and what they mean

A quick guide to common symptoms, with a link to the service that fixes each one.

Door not opening

The cause is often a broken spring, a dead remote battery, a disconnected opener or misaligned sensors. If the opener runs but the door stays down, a spring may have failed. Do not force the door.

Not-opening service

Door not closing

Dirty or misaligned safety sensors are a frequent cause. Wipe the lenses and check the indicator lights. If the door still stops or reverses, have the sensors and travel limits inspected.

Not-closing service

Broken spring

A loud bang, a visible gap in the coil, or a door that feels extremely heavy points to a broken spring. Stop using the door and avoid the spring area. Replacement is a job for a trained technician.

Spring replacement

Cable coming off the drum

A cable that has slipped off the drum often leaves one side of the door hanging low. Do not run the opener. Cables are under tension and should be re-seated or replaced by a professional.

Cable off-drum repair

Unusual noises

Grinding often means worn rollers, squeaks point to dry hinges or bearings, and banging can mean loose hardware or an unbalanced door. A diagnosis finds the part responsible.

Noisy door repair

Uneven movement

A door that rises crooked or shakes may have a worn cable, uneven spring tension, bent track or damaged hinge. Continued use can cause further damage, so schedule an inspection.

Uneven-opening repair

Opener not responding

Check the remote battery and that the opener has power. If it still ignores the remote, wall button or keypad, the logic board, receiver or motor may need service.

Opener repair

Sensor malfunction

Blinking lights, a door that will not close, or reversing before it touches the floor suggest sensor trouble. Clean the lenses gently. Persistent faults may mean a damaged wire or failed unit.

Sensor replacement

Safety first

Springs, cables and bottom brackets are under extreme tension. Do not adjust, loosen or remove them yourself. Stop using the door and call a professional.
