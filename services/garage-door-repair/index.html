What does garage door repair include?

Garage door repair covers the parts that lift, guide and power your door: springs and counterbalance systems, cables and drums, tracks and rollers, hinges and brackets, door alignment, openers and safety sensors. Technicians also correct operating faults such as a door that sticks, reverses or runs unevenly. KSE Door Mechanics provides this service in Boston, MA; call (833) 877-2561.

Several different failures can look the same from the driveway, so diagnosis comes before the repair. Replacing the wrong part wastes money and can leave a hazard in place. Use this table as a starting point, not a substitute for inspection.

Common garage door problems we repair

Garage door won’t open or close

The cause is often a failed spring, an opener fault, blocked or misaligned safety sensors, or a binding track. Each needs a different fix, so guessing wastes time.

Common signs: Opener hums but the door stays down, door reverses, or it moves a few inches and stops.

Our approach: We inspect balance, springs, cables, tracks, sensors and the opener before recommending a repair. Don’t force a stuck door.

Door not opening Door not closing

Broken garage door spring

Torsion springs sit above the door; extension springs run along the tracks. Both counterbalance the door’s weight, so a broken one makes it feel very heavy.

Common signs: A loud bang, a visible gap in the coil, a door that won’t lift, or a crooked, uneven door.

Our approach: Springs are replaced by a trained technician with the right tools, then balance and operation are checked.

Spring replacement

Garage door opener problems

Openers fail in the motor, drive, logic board, wall control, remote or sensors. Many faults can be fixed without replacing the whole unit.

Common signs: Remote works only up close, motor runs without moving the door, lights blink, or the door reverses.

Our approach: We test the controls and safety sensors first, then repair or replace the failed part and re-test reversal.

Opener repair

Garage door cable problems

Lift cables wind on drums and carry the door’s load. Fraying, rust or a cable that slips off its drum lets one side drop.

Common signs: Frayed strands, a loose cable, a door hanging crooked, or a cable visible outside the drum.

Our approach: We replace worn cables and check the drums, brackets and spring balance together. Never touch a loaded cable.

Cable repair

Garage door roller and track problems

Worn rollers, bent or misaligned tracks and a door that has left its track cause noise, jerky travel and extra strain on the opener.

Common signs: Grinding, scraping, rattling, a door that sticks mid-travel, or a gap between roller and track.

Our approach: We replace worn rollers, realign tracks and repair or replace damaged track sections.

Track repair Roller replacement

Garage door hinge and hardware problems

Hinges, brackets, bearings and struts hold the panels together and keep the door aligned. Loose or worn hardware shows up as sagging or racking.

Common signs: Cracked hinges, panels pulling apart, a bowed door, squealing bearings or loose fasteners.

Our approach: We replace worn hardware and realign the door so load spreads evenly across the sections.

Hinge replacement

Should You Repair or Replace Your Garage Door?

Repair is usually enough when one component has worn out on an otherwise sound door. Replacement is worth considering when the door itself is failing or keeps breaking down. We explain both options after inspection, before work starts.

Garage door safety

Springs store substantial mechanical energy, and cables and drums operate under tension. A damaged or unbalanced door can move unexpectedly, and a faulty opener may not protect against that movement. Safety sensors and auto-reverse should be tested whenever the door or opener is repaired.

If your door is visibly damaged, crooked or off its track, stop using it, keep people and vehicles clear, and call a professional. Do not attempt to wind, unwind or adjust springs, release cables, remove bottom brackets, force a stuck door or bypass opener safety features.

Garage door repair FAQs

What garage door problems can KSE Door Mechanics repair?

We repair springs, cables and drums, tracks and rollers, hinges and brackets, door balance and alignment, and garage door openers including motors, logic boards, remotes, keypads and safety sensors. Call (833) 877-2561 to describe your problem and ask whether it is something we can service.

Why won’t my garage door open?

Common causes are a broken spring, a snapped cable, a disconnected opener, a dead remote battery or misaligned safety sensors. If the opener runs but the door stays down, a spring is a likely cause. Don’t force the door; call for an inspection.

Why does my garage door stop halfway?

A door that stops partway usually has uneven balance, a binding roller or track, a worn spring, or opener limit or force settings that need adjustment. Because several causes look alike, a technician should check balance and hardware before changing opener settings.

Can a broken garage door spring be repaired or replaced?

Broken springs are normally replaced rather than patched. Springs wear in cycles, so technicians often recommend replacing them as a matched set and checking the cables and balance at the same time. Spring work should be done by trained professionals.

Is it safe to operate a garage door with a broken spring?

No. Without its spring, the door is much heavier than the opener is designed to lift, which can strain the opener and make the door move unpredictably. Stop using it, keep people and vehicles clear, and call for service.

What causes garage door cables to break?

Cables fray with age, wear and rust, and can be damaged when a door runs out of balance or a roller or drum is misaligned. Road-salt moisture can speed corrosion. Frayed or loose cables should be inspected promptly and never handled while the door is loaded.

Can an off-track garage door be repaired?

Often, yes. Depending on the damage, a technician may realign the track and rollers, replace worn rollers, or replace bent track sections. Don’t try to push the door back into place, because it may be unbalanced or unsupported.

Why is my garage door making grinding noises?

Grinding often points to worn rollers, dry or worn hinges, bearings, a misaligned track or an unbalanced door. Noise is an early warning, so an inspection can catch a small repair before it grows into a larger one.

Do you repair garage door openers?

Yes. We repair openers by diagnosing the motor, drive, logic board, wall control, remote and safety sensors, and we also program remotes and keypads. Repair is often possible without replacing the whole door, and sometimes the opener itself.

Can I replace one garage door panel instead of the entire door?

Sometimes. Replacing a single section may be possible for a product-related or mechanical defect when a matching section is available. Matching the model, size and design can be difficult on older doors, so the technician will confirm before recommending it.

How do I know whether to repair or replace my garage door?

Repair usually fits a sound door with one worn component. Replacement is worth considering when panels are failing, parts are hard to find, several components are worn or the same door keeps failing. A technician can compare both options after inspecting it.
