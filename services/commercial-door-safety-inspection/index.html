Safety: what to leave to professionals

Commercial doors are heavy and their springs, cables and operators are under high tension, so only trained technicians should work on them.

Safe choices

  • Tell us how often the door cycles and what it protects.
  • Keep the area clear of vehicles and people during service.
  • Share access, security and safety rules in advance.

Do not

  • Do not operate a door with a damaged spring, cable or track.
  • Do not bypass safety edges or photo-eyes.
  • Do not let untrained staff adjust high-tension parts.
