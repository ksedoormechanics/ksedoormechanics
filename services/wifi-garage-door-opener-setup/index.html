Safety: what to leave to professionals

Installing an opener means electrical work, structural mounting and setting safety systems, and the door must be balanced first.

Safe choices

  • Have the door balanced before the opener goes in.
  • Confirm the opener suits your door and space.
  • Ask for safety reversal testing at the end.

Do not

  • Do not install an opener on an unbalanced door.
  • Do not skip or bypass the safety sensors.
  • Do not mount an opener to unsupported framing.
